About

Not “read the book for you.” Understand what is useful faster.

Essence is an independent knowledge base built around ideas, problems and actions rather than compressed chapter-by-chapter retellings.

How each guide works

We isolate several central ideas, explain them in original wording, add context and turn them into practical tests. The structure of the original book is not reproduced.

01Meaning

What the idea is actually trying to explain and when it is useful.

02Connections

Which other books and concepts strengthen, challenge or clarify it.

03Action

What you can test in real life instead of collecting another quote.

What you will not find here

No full copyrighted texts, scans, EPUB/PDF files, copied covers or sequential chapter-by-chapter substitutes for the original work.

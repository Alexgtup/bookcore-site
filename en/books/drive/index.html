Drive

Daniel H. Pink · 2009

An argument that autonomy, mastery and purpose often motivate knowledge work better than simple external rewards.

#motivation#autonomy#mastery
Key ideas ↓Русская версия
In one sentence

Design environments where people have meaningful control, can improve their craft and understand why the work matters.

Why it mattersCore ideasTry it

Why it matters

In complex work, controlling incentives can narrow attention or weaken intrinsic engagement.

Core ideas

What is worth remembering after you close the page

01

Autonomy supports ownership

Control over how work is done can increase responsibility rather than reduce it.

02

Mastery needs difficulty

People stay engaged when the work is challenging enough to invite growth.

03

Purpose connects effort

A credible reason beyond the immediate task can sustain motivation.

03
Turn it into action

Three things to test today

  1. 1

    Give yourself or a teammate one real choice over how a task is completed.

  2. 2

    Choose a skill edge to practice deliberately this week.

  3. 3

    Connect one routine task to the user or outcome it serves.

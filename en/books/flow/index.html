Flow

Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi · 1990

An account of deep absorption that emerges when challenge and skill are balanced around clear goals and immediate feedback.

Key ideas ↓Русская версия
In one sentence

Design work so difficulty stretches your ability without making progress feel impossible.

Why it matters

Engagement often grows from the structure of an activity rather than from passive comfort.

Core ideas

What is worth remembering after you close the page

01

Balance challenge and skill

Boredom appears when challenge is too low; anxiety rises when it is too high.

02

Clear goals narrow attention

A specific target reduces the mental noise around an activity.

03

Feedback sustains immersion

Visible progress helps attention stay connected to the task.

03
Turn it into action

Three things to test today

  1. 1

    Raise or lower the difficulty of one task to match your current skill.

  2. 2

    Define a visible goal for the next hour.

  3. 3

    Create a quick feedback loop so you can see progress while working.

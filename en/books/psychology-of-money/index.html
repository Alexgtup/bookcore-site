The Psychology of Money

Morgan Housel · 2020

A behavioral view of wealth, risk and financial decisions that explains why temperament often matters more than formulas.

Enough is a strategy

Knowing what is sufficient protects you from taking unnecessary risks for marginal gains.

Room for error

Mindset

Carol Dweck · 2006

A framework for how beliefs about ability can influence effort, feedback, persistence and learning behavior.

Evaluate yourself by the quality of the learning process, not by whether every attempt looks talented.

What is worth remembering after you close the page

01

Ability can develop

Skills are not infinitely malleable, but many improve substantially through practice and strategy.

02

Process praise matters

Rewarding effective effort and adaptation encourages better learning behavior than protecting an identity.

03

Feedback is information

Criticism becomes more useful when separated from self-worth.

Three things to test today

    Replace one 'I am bad at this' statement with a skill-specific diagnosis.

  2. 2

    Ask for one piece of actionable feedback.

  3. 3

    Track strategy changes, not just outcomes, for a week.

Zero to One

Peter Thiel · 2014

A contrarian startup argument for creating unique value instead of competing inside an already crowded market.

Key ideas ↓Русская версия
In one sentence

Look for a valuable truth or capability that lets you build something meaningfully different.

Why it mattersCore ideasTry it
01
Why it matters

A company can capture more value when it solves a real problem in a differentiated way rather than merely copying incumbents.

02
Core ideas

What is worth remembering after you close the page

01

Difference creates leverage

Moving from zero to one means creating a new capability, not scaling an existing pattern.

02

Competition can erase value

Crowded markets can force companies to spend their advantage fighting each other.

03

Start narrow

Dominating a small useful niche can be a stronger foundation than serving everyone weakly.

03
Turn it into action

Three things to test today

  1. 1

    Name the narrowest market where your product could be the obvious choice.

  2. 2

    Write one non-obvious belief behind your product thesis.

  3. 3

    Remove one feature that exists only because competitors have it.

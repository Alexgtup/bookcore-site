The Lean Startup

Eric Ries · 2011

A method for reducing startup uncertainty through explicit hypotheses, small experiments and validated learning.

#startup#experiments#product

Treat product development as a sequence of learning loops, not a long march toward an assumed answer.

What is worth remembering after you close the page

01

Build–measure–learn

Each product iteration should be designed to test a specific uncertainty.

02

Learning is the output

Early progress is better measured by reduced uncertainty than by feature count.

03

Pivot with evidence

A pivot changes the strategy while preserving what you learned about the goal.

Turn it into action

Three things to test today

  1. 1

    Write the riskiest assumption behind your current idea.

  2. 2

    Design the cheapest test that could disprove it.

  3. 3

    Decide in advance what result would cause you to change direction.

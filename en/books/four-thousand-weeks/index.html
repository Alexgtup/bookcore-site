Four Thousand Weeks

Oliver Burkeman · 2021

A challenge to productivity obsession built around the fact that a human life contains a sharply limited number of weeks.

Key ideas ↓Русская версия
In one sentence

Meaningful time management begins by choosing what you are willing not to do.

01

You cannot fit everything

Efficiency does not abolish finitude; it often attracts more demands.

02

Choose your neglect

A good life requires deciding which worthy things will remain undone.

03

Be where your time is

Constant optimization can become a way of postponing actual experience.

Turn it into action

Three things to test today

  1. 1

    Pick three commitments that deserve your finite attention this month.

  2. 2

    Create a deliberate 'not now' list.

  3. 3

    Spend one hour on an activity without trying to optimize or document it.

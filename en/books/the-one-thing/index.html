The ONE Thing

Gary Keller & Jay Papasan · 2013

A prioritization method centered on finding the single action that makes other work easier, smaller or unnecessary.

#focus#priority#leverage
Key ideas ↓Русская версия
In one sentence

Find the highest-leverage next action and protect time for it before lower-value work expands.

What is worth remembering after you close the page

01

Priority is singular

The word loses meaning when everything is treated as equally urgent.

02

Leverage changes the list

The best task can remove, simplify or accelerate many later tasks.

03

Time-block the important

A priority without protected time becomes a preference.

Turn it into action

Three things to test today

  1. 1

    Ask what one action would make the rest easier or unnecessary.

  2. 2

    Block two hours for it before opening messages.

  3. 3

    Remove three low-value tasks from today's list.

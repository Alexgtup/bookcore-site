Just Do It. Keep It Simple.

Oscar Hartmann · 2019

Oscar Hartmann's action-oriented argument for replacing endless preparation with repeated attempts, feedback and forward motion.

Treat action as a trainable skill: shorten the distance between intention and the next attempt.

Three things to test today

  1. 1

    Choose a goal you have been preparing for and make one real attempt today.

  2. 2

    Write the smallest next move that produces external feedback.

  3. 3

    After the attempt, record one lesson and immediately define the next test.

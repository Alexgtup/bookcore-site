Never Split the Difference

Chris Voss · 2016

A tactical negotiation framework built around calibrated questions, labeling emotions and uncovering hidden constraints.

Create enough understanding to reveal the real problem before bargaining over the visible position.

01
Why it matters

Negotiations are rarely solved by arithmetic alone; the other side's perceptions and unspoken limits shape the range of possible agreements.

02
Core ideas

What is worth remembering after you close the page

01

Label what you hear

Naming an emotion or dynamic can lower defensiveness and surface missing information.

02

Ask calibrated questions

Open 'how' and 'what' questions can make the other side engage with the problem.

03

No can create safety

People may feel more control when they can reject a framing instead of being pushed toward yes.

03
Turn it into action

Three things to test today

  1. 1

    Prepare three 'what' or 'how' questions before your next negotiation.

  2. 2

    Summarize the other side's position until they say you understand it.

  3. 3

    Find the constraint behind one stated demand before discussing price or terms.

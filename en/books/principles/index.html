Principles

Ray Dalio · 2017

A system for turning repeated decisions into explicit principles, testing them against reality and improving them over time.

Key ideas ↓Русская версия
In one sentence

Write principles only after observing real decisions, then revise them when evidence shows the rule is wrong.

Why it matters

Codified rules can reduce inconsistency and make reasoning easier to inspect, challenge and transfer.

Core ideas

What is worth remembering after you close the page

01

Make reasoning explicit

Written principles expose assumptions that otherwise stay hidden in intuition.

02

Use mistakes as data

Painful outcomes are most valuable when traced back to a process that can be redesigned.

03

Match people and roles

Good systems account for differences in strengths rather than assuming everyone should operate the same way.

03
Turn it into action

Three things to test today

  1. 1

    Write one recurring decision rule you currently use implicitly.

  2. 2

    Test it against three past examples.

  3. 3

    Change the rule if it predicts the wrong behavior more than once.

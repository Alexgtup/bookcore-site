Thinking, Fast and Slow

Daniel Kahneman · 2011

A map of fast intuition, slow reasoning and the predictable biases that shape judgment under uncertainty.

Two modes of thought

Biases are systematic

Three things to test today

  1. 1

    For one high-stakes choice, list the base rate before your personal story.

  2. 2

    Ask what information would change your mind.

  3. 3

    Separate confidence in the story from quality of the evidence.

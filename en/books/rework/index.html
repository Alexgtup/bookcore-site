Rework

Jason Fried & David Heinemeier Hansson · 2010

A collection of deliberately simple operating principles for building products with less ceremony, fewer assumptions and faster feedback.

Key ideas ↓Русская версия
In one sentence

Prefer direct work, small scope and real customers over organizational theater.

    Cut one planned feature that is not necessary for the core outcome.

  2. 2

    Replace one recurring meeting with an asynchronous update.

  3. 3

    Ship one smaller version to real users sooner.

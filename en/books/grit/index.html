Grit

Angela Duckworth · 2016

A case for sustained interest and effort over long horizons, balanced by deliberate practice and adaptive goals.

Effort compounds talent

Skill improves through repeated effort, and achievement requires applying that skill again.

02

Practice needs feedback

Mere repetition is weaker than focused work on a specific weakness.

Hierarchy matters

    Identify the one subskill limiting your progress.

  2. 2

  3. 3

    Separate a tactical setback from the higher-level goal it was serving.

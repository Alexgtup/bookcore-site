Antifragile

Nassim Nicholas Taleb · 2012

A theory of systems that do more than resist shocks: they can improve when exposed to bounded volatility and stress.

Key ideas ↓Русская версия
In one sentence

Limit ruin, keep upside open, and prefer systems that learn from small stress instead of hiding it.

Why it matters

Optimization for stable forecasts can create hidden fragility when the environment is uncertain.

Core ideas

What is worth remembering after you close the page

01

Fragility hates volatility

Some systems break when variation increases even if the average looks unchanged.

02

Small stress can teach

Frequent bounded errors can reveal weakness before it becomes catastrophic.

03

Avoid ruin first

Upside does not compensate for strategies that contain a meaningful chance of irreversible loss.

03
Turn it into action

Three things to test today

  1. 1

    Identify one single point of failure in a current plan.

  2. 2

    Replace one large irreversible bet with smaller experiments.

  3. 3

    Keep a reserve that lets you survive being wrong longer than expected.

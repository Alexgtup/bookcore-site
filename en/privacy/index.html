Transparency

Privacy policy

BookCore collects only the technical data needed to operate the site, understand real traffic and improve the editorial product.

Analytics

The site uses Yandex Metrica (counter 113020098). The service may process technical information about the browser, device, referral source, pages viewed and interactions with the site.

Cookies and local storage

Analytics services may use cookies or similar technologies. Local browser storage is also used for product features such as saved books and reading state.

Advertising

Yandex Advertising Network technology is technically integrated with the site. Ad serving becomes active only after the property passes platform review. When ads are active, Yandex may use cookies and other technical identifiers for ad selection, frequency control and measurement. Visitors can manage cookies and personalization through their browser and Yandex settings.

Affiliate links

Some pages may contain affiliate materials from book services, including Litres. If a visitor follows an affiliate link and makes a purchase, the project may receive a commission. Affiliate placement does not change the editorial conclusions of the guide.

Infrastructure

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages and may use Cloudflare infrastructure to deliver content and protect connections.
